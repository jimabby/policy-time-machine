# Expense Reimbursement Policy — v1 (in force)

## 1. Receipts
1.1 A valid receipt must be attached for any claim over **GBP 25**. Claims over
    this threshold without a receipt are **denied**.

## 2. Meals
2.1 Meals are reimbursed up to **GBP 40 per day**. Claims above the cap are
    reimbursed **up to the cap only** (a partial reimbursement), not denied.

## 3. Travel
3.1 Travel booked with **fewer than 7 days' notice** requires prior written
    approval from a director. Without it the claim is **denied**.

## 4. Entertainment
4.1 Alcohol is **not reimbursable** under any circumstance. Where a claim
    includes alcohol, the non-alcohol portion is reimbursed (a partial).

## 5. Authorisation
5.1 Any claim over **GBP 500** requires prior director approval. Without it the
    claim is **denied**.

## 6. Discretion
6.1 Nothing in this policy prevents a director from approving an otherwise
    non-compliant claim in writing, but the reason must be recorded.
