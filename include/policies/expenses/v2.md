# Expense Reimbursement Policy — v2 (PROPOSED, not in force)

Rationale for the change: the finance team spends more on chasing receipts for
small claims than the claims are worth, and short-notice client travel is
routinely penalised even when the client caused it.

## 1. Receipts
1.1 A valid receipt must be attached for any claim over **GBP 75** (raised from
    GBP 25). Claims over this threshold without a receipt are **denied**.

## 2. Meals
2.1 Meals are reimbursed up to **GBP 60 per day** (raised from GBP 40). Claims
    above the cap are reimbursed **up to the cap only**, not denied.

## 3. Travel
3.1 Travel booked with fewer than 7 days' notice requires prior written
    approval from a director, **unless the short notice was client-driven and
    the employee has recorded the reason**, in which case the claim is
    approved.

## 4. Entertainment
4.1 Alcohol remains **not reimbursable**. Where a claim includes alcohol, the
    non-alcohol portion is reimbursed (a partial).

## 5. Authorisation
5.1 Any claim over **GBP 1000** (raised from GBP 500) requires prior director
    approval. Without it the claim is **denied**.

## 6. Seniority exemption (new)
6.1 Employees at **grade 3 or above** are exempt from the receipt requirement in
    clause 1.1, on the basis that their claims are reviewed at budget level.

## 7. Discretion
7.1 Unchanged from v1.
