# Membership Cancellation & Refund Policy — v2 (PROPOSED)

Rationale: forfeiting the balance of long-standing members generates more
complaints and chargebacks than it retains revenue.

## 1. Notice
1.1 Members must give **14 days' notice** (reduced from 30). With less notice,
    the final month is charged (a partial).

## 2. Minimum term
2.1 Cancelling inside the minimum term forfeits the remaining balance — **no
    refund** — unless clause 3 or clause 5 applies.

## 3. Exceptions
3.1 A **documented medical** reason waives the forfeit in full.
3.2 **Relocation over 15 miles** (reduced from 25), with documentation, waives
    the forfeit in full.
3.3 Undocumented claims do not qualify.

## 4. Outside the minimum term
4.1 Unchanged — full refund on proper notice.

## 5. Loyalty waiver (new)
5.1 Members on the **premium tier** are exempt from the clause 2.1 forfeit
    regardless of how much of the minimum term remains.
