# Membership Cancellation & Refund Policy — v1 (in force)

## 1. Notice
1.1 Members must give **30 days' notice**. With less notice, the final month
    is charged and only the balance beyond it is refundable (a partial).

## 2. Minimum term
2.1 Cancelling **inside a 12-month minimum term** forfeits the remaining
    balance — **no refund** — unless clause 3 applies.

## 3. Exceptions
3.1 A **documented medical** reason waives the clause 2.1 forfeit in full.
3.2 **Relocation over 25 miles**, with documentation, waives the forfeit in full.
3.3 Undocumented claims under 3.1 or 3.2 do **not** qualify.

## 4. Outside the minimum term
4.1 Members past their minimum term who give proper notice receive a **full
    refund** of any balance.
